We Are Proudly IICRC Certified

The IICRC — the Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification — is the most recognized credentialing body in the carpet, cleaning and restoration industry. Technicians must pass technical training and follow professional standards to earn and keep that certification.

That certification is not a marketing badge. It is the result of real training — and it shows in the work we deliver on every job. Being fully insured protects you: if anything unexpected happens on your property, you are covered.

Our Pledge to You

Keeping our word is just how we do business. We promise to:

  • Re-clean absolutely free if you are not 100% satisfied
  • Dispose of wastewater in an environmentally safe way, in compliance with local, state and federal regulations
  • Never negatively affect the stain resistance, wear or appearance of your carpeting
  • Use only cleaning solutions that are safe, biodegradable and not tested on animals
