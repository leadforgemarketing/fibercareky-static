FAQs

Common Questions, Straight Answers

How often should I have my carpets professionally cleaned?

It depends on foot traffic, pets and children, but most Louisville homes benefit from professional cleaning every 6–12 months. Homes with pets or allergy sufferers should lean toward every 6 months.

How long does carpet take to dry after cleaning?

Typically 6–12 hours depending on humidity, carpet thickness and airflow. We use high-powered extraction to remove as much moisture as possible, and we will give you tips to speed drying before we leave.

Are your cleaning products safe for children and pets?

Yes. We use non-toxic, eco-friendly, biodegradable cleaning solutions. They are safe for your whole family — including the four-legged members.

Can you remove pet urine stains and odors?

In most cases, yes. We use enzyme-based treatments and deep extraction that target the odor-causing bacteria in the carpet backing and padding, not just the surface. Severe long-term contamination may need pad replacement — we will always tell you honestly.

Do you really respond 24/7 for water damage?

Yes. Water damage gets worse by the hour and mold can start within 24–48 hours, so our emergency line is answered around the clock, every day of the year.

Do you serve my area?

We cover all of Louisville and Jefferson County plus Oldham, Shelby and Bullitt counties — including Jeffersontown, St. Matthews, Middletown, Anchorage, Prospect, Crestwood and La Grange. Call to confirm your neighborhood.
