Your Furniture Deserves Better Than a Guess

Your furniture is a real investment — in money and in sentiment. Over time, upholstery accumulates dirt, allergens, stains and odors that make it look dull and feel tired. But every fabric is different: what revives a microfiber sectional can ruin a silk cushion.

Our technicians assess fabric codes and fiber type before selecting any cleaning method — so your sofa, sectional or office chair gets the right treatment, not a guess. We safely handle fabrics that cannot go to a dry cleaner.

  • Sofas, sectionals, armchairs, dining chairs, ottomans
  • Silk, leather, microfiber, cotton, synthetics and blends
  • Pet odor and allergen removal built in
  • Eco-friendly products safe around kids and pets

Our Upholstery Cleaning Process

Every piece starts with a fabric and dye-stability assessment. We pre-treat spots and high-contact areas, then clean with controlled moisture and professional extraction matched to the fabric — powerful enough to lift embedded soil, gentle enough to protect the material. Finally we groom the fabric and speed drying with air movement, so most pieces are ready to use the same day.

Allergens, Odors and Pet Problems

Upholstery traps dust mites, pollen, dander and odors just like carpet does. Our high-powered extraction removes allergens at the source, and enzyme treatments neutralize pet odors in the fabric — a genuinely healthier sit, not a perfumed cover-up.

FAQs

Upholstery Cleaning Questions

How long does upholstery take to dry?

Most pieces are dry within 2–6 hours. We use controlled moisture and speed drying with air movers where needed, and we will tell you exactly what to expect for your fabric.

Can you clean delicate fabrics like silk or velvet?

Yes — that is exactly why we assess fabric codes first. Delicate fibers get low-moisture or specialist methods matched to their tolerance.

Will cleaning fade my furniture?

No. We test dye stability before cleaning and use pH-appropriate, eco-friendly solutions. Your fabric's color and texture are protected throughout.
