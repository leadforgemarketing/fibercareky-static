Signs You Need a Mold Assessment

  • A persistent musty, earthy smell — especially in the basement or after rain
  • Visible black, green or white spotting on walls, ceilings, grout or around windows
  • Allergy symptoms that ease when you leave the house
  • Past flooding, leaks or condensation problems — even years old
  • Peeling paint or warped drywall (hidden moisture behind the surface)

Our Mold Remediation Process

Assessment & Moisture Mapping

We inspect visible growth and hunt the hidden kind — behind walls, under flooring, in HVAC ducts — using moisture meters to map the true extent. You get a straight answer and a written estimate.

Containment

The work area is sealed off so spores cannot spread to clean areas of your home during removal. Negative air and physical barriers keep the rest of your house safe.

Removal & Treatment

Contaminated porous materials (drywall, insulation, carpet) are removed and disposed of properly. Salvageable surfaces are treated with eco-conscious antimicrobial solutions that kill mold at the root.

Drying & Source Repair

We dry the structure to verified moisture targets and identify the water source — leak, condensation, drainage or humidity — with clear recommendations to fix it for good.

Final Inspection & Prevention Plan

We walk the job with you, confirm the area is clean and dry, and leave you with a practical prevention plan: ventilation, dehumidification and maintenance tailored to your home.

FAQs

Mold Questions, Straight Answers

How do I know if I have a mold problem?

Musty odors, visible spots or discoloration on walls or ceilings, worsening allergies indoors, or any recent water damage are the classic signs. If you suspect mold, do not disturb it — call for an assessment.

Is mold dangerous?

Some molds trigger allergies and respiratory irritation, and certain species are more hazardous, especially for children, the elderly and anyone with asthma or compromised immunity. The EPA recommends professional remediation for areas larger than about 10 square feet.

Can I just clean mold with bleach?

Bleach lightens the stain on non-porous surfaces but rarely kills the root structure in porous materials like drywall and wood — and disturbing a colony without containment spreads spores through your home. Professional remediation addresses the source and the moisture that feeds it.

How much does mold remediation cost in Louisville?

It depends on the size and location of the colony and whether materials need removal. Small contained jobs run a few hundred dollars; whole-basement remediation costs more. We assess first and give you an honest written estimate before any work begins.

Will the mold come back?

Mold returns only if the moisture source returns. That is why every FiberCare remediation includes moisture-source identification and prevention recommendations — we fix the cause, not just the symptom.
