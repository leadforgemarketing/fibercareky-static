The FiberCare Difference

Why Louisville Carpets Need More Than a Vacuum

Vacuuming is essential — but it only touches the top layer. Beneath the surface, compacted soil, allergens, bacteria and moisture-borne contaminants build up inside the fiber pile over time. That hidden soil is what dulls colors, flattens traffic lanes and wears your carpet out early.

Our truck-mounted hot water extraction reaches the base of the fiber, flushing out what household machines leave behind — then pulls the moisture back out so your carpet dries fast and stays cleaner longer.

  • Hot water extraction — the method carpet manufacturers recommend
  • No soil-attracting residue — carpets stay cleaner, longer
  • High-temperature cleaning kills bacteria and germs
  • Safe, biodegradable solutions — no phosphates, no harsh solvents

FAQs

Carpet Cleaning Questions

How much does carpet cleaning cost in Louisville?

Most homes run between $120 and $300 depending on the number of rooms, their size, and condition. We quote a real price up front — the number we give you on the phone is the number you pay.

How long until I can walk on my carpet?

You can walk on it right away with clean socks or shoe covers. Full dry time is typically 6–12 hours depending on humidity and airflow.

Can you get out old, set-in stains?

Often, yes. Our technicians identify the stain type and use targeted treatments. Some stains (bleach spots, certain dyes) are permanent — and we will tell you honestly before you pay us to try.

Do you move furniture?

We move standard furniture like sofas, chairs and tables, clean underneath, and put everything back on protective pads. Large items like beds and entertainment centers can be worked around or moved by arrangement.

Is hot water extraction the same as steam cleaning?

It is what most people mean by 'steam cleaning' — hot water and cleaning solution are injected deep into the pile and immediately extracted with the dirt. It is the method carpet manufacturers recommend, and the only one that reaches the base of the fiber.
