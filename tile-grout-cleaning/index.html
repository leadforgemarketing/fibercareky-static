Mopping Moves Dirt. We Remove It.

Mopping pushes dirty water into grout lines, where it dries and darkens them a little more every time. Household products cannot reach what is compacted inside porous grout — but our high-pressure professional equipment can.

We restore kitchen, bathroom and entryway tile that household cleaning has given up on — ceramic, porcelain and natural stone — then seal the grout afterward to keep it cleaner, longer.

Our Tile & Grout Cleaning Process

  • Pre-treatment to break down embedded grime, mold and mildew
  • High-pressure cleaning and extraction that pulls dirt out of grout lines
  • Detailing of edges, corners and transitions

FAQs

Tile & Grout Cleaning Questions

Can you really get grout white again?

In most cases, yes — the dark color is compacted soil, and high-pressure extraction removes it. Permanently stained or damaged grout may need color sealing, which we also offer.

Is your process safe for natural stone?

Yes. Natural stone gets pH-appropriate products and adjusted pressure. We identify your tile type before we touch it.

Should I seal my grout after cleaning?

We recommend it. Sealing fills the grout's pores so spills and mop water bead on top instead of soaking in — it keeps your tile looking freshly cleaned far longer.
