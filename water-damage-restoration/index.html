Fast, Certified Water Damage Restoration for Louisville Homes & Businesses

Whether it is a burst pipe at 2am, a flooded basement after a storm, or a slow leak you just discovered behind a wall, water damage demands one thing above all else: speed. Water seeps into walls, floors, furniture and belongings within minutes — and mold begins colonizing damp materials within 24–48 hours.

FiberCare Cleaning & Restoration has restored water-damaged properties across Louisville and Jefferson County for decades. Our IICRC-certified technicians arrive with truck-mounted extraction units, commercial dehumidifiers and air movers — and a plan.

Four Emergency Steps to Take Right Now

  1. Stop the source. Locate your main water valve and shut it off. If water has reached outlets or appliances, cut power at the breaker — only if it is safe to do so.
  2. Remove standing water. A wet/dry vacuum, mops and towels can slow the spread while you wait for us. Every gallon you remove now is less damage later.
  3. Move belongings to a dry area. Get furniture, rugs and valuables out of the affected zone. Aluminum foil under furniture legs prevents staining.
  4. Call us, then your insurance company. The restoration clock is ticking. We can be on our way while you start your claim.

Our Water Damage Restoration Process

Emergency Contact & Assessment

You call, we answer — day or night. On arrival we assess the damage, identify the water category (clean, gray or black water) and map the moisture with professional meters, including inside walls and under flooring.

Water Extraction

Truck-mounted pumps and industrial extractors remove standing water fast — from carpet, padding, hardwood, tile and concrete. Rapid extraction is the single biggest factor in limiting damage.

Structural Drying & Dehumidification

Commercial air movers and dehumidifiers pull moisture from walls, subfloors and framing. We monitor daily with moisture meters and dry to verified targets (below 16% moisture content) — not guesswork.

Cleaning, Sanitizing & Mold Prevention

Affected areas are cleaned and sanitized with eco-friendly antimicrobials. We apply mold-prevention treatment to at-risk materials so a water problem does not become a mold problem.

Restoration & Final Inspection

We repair or coordinate the repair of damaged drywall, flooring and trim, then walk the property with you for a final inspection. The job is not done until your home is back to pre-loss condition.

Mold Prevention & Remediation

Water damage and mold go hand in hand in Louisville's humid climate. Every water damage job includes a mold-risk assessment, and our mold remediation team handles colonies that have already taken hold — safely, thoroughly, and with prevention built in.

We Work With Your Insurance Company

Dealing with insurance during a crisis is overwhelming. We document damage with photos and moisture readings, provide the detailed estimates adjusters need, and communicate directly with your provider to keep the claims process moving.

FAQs

Water Damage Questions, Answered

How fast can you get here for a water emergency?

We answer our emergency line 24/7 and dispatch as fast as possible across Louisville and the eastern suburbs. In a water emergency, minutes matter — call (502) 491-1242 immediately.

Will my homeowner's insurance cover water damage restoration?

Most policies cover sudden and accidental water damage (burst pipes, appliance failures). We work directly with insurance companies and can help with documentation, photos and the claims process.

How long does the drying process take?

Most homes take 3–5 days of professional drying with commercial dehumidifiers and air movers. We monitor moisture levels daily and do not remove equipment until readings confirm materials are dry (below 16% moisture content).

Can wet carpet be saved?

Often yes, if we extract quickly and the water was clean (Category 1). Sewage-contaminated or long-soaked carpet and padding usually must be replaced — we will give you an honest assessment, not a sales pitch.

Do I really need professionals, or can I dry it myself?

Shop vacuums and household fans handle surface water, but moisture trapped in wall cavities, subfloors and padding breeds mold within 24–48 hours. Professional extraction and structural drying is the difference between a cleanup and a $10,000 mold problem.
