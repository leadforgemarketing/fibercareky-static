We Use Eco-Friendly Cleaning Products Only

Unlike other cleaning systems, our solutions are free of phosphates and non-processable solvents. That means no soil-attracting residue is left behind — and your carpets stay cleaner longer. It also means our solutions are safe around the people and pets who matter most.

Every product we bring into your home is biodegradable, non-toxic and never tested on animals.

Steam Does the Heavy Lifting

Believe it or not, the best way to get rid of grime, odors and allergens is steam. Many methods use chemical particulates to lift dirt — leaving residue that wears your carpet down over time. High-temperature steam leaves nothing behind. No dirt. No residue. No harsh chemicals.

Heat Kills What Chemicals Can't

We let high temperatures do a lot of the work. Cleaning at higher temperatures kills bacteria and germs while reducing the amount of cleaning solution we need — a win for your home and for the environment.

Safe Protectors & Deodorizers

We use Scotchgard™ products and specially formulated deodorizers because they are the safest, most effective on the market — keeping stains and odors from coming back between professional cleanings.

Hot Water Extraction: The Method Manufacturers Recommend

Truck-mounted hot water extraction injects heated water and cleaning solution deep into the carpet pile, then immediately extracts it — along with compacted soil, allergens and bacteria. It is the only method that reaches the base of the fiber, and the one carpet manufacturers recommend to protect your warranty.
