About FiberCare Cleaning & Restoration

FiberCare Cleaning & Restoration is a family-run cleaning and restoration company based on Shelbyville Road in Louisville, Kentucky. In business since 1993, FiberCare provides emergency water damage restoration, mold remediation and professional floor and fabric cleaning to homes and businesses across the Louisville metro.
